Our Manifesto

“Imperfection is where the human soul hides.”

We reject the mechanical perfection of mass assembly. In our studio, every curve begins as a wax model shaped by hand, left with the subtle fingerprint of the artisan who carved it.

We work exclusively with certified 100% recycled gold and ethically unearthed gemstones whose natural inclusions are celebrated as geological memories, not flaws to be cut away.
